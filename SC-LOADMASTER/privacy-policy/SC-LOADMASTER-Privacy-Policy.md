# Privacy Policy for SC-LOADMASTER

**Last Updated:** February 14, 2026  
**Effective Date:** February 25, 2026

---

## Introduction

SC-LOADMASTER ("the App") is designed to help Star Citizen players manage cargo transportation contracts. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it's used, and your rights regarding your data.

**The short version:** SC-LOADMASTER stores all data locally on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or share any of your personal information or app usage data.

---

## Information We Collect

### Data You Create in the App

SC-LOADMASTER stores the following information **locally on your device only**:

1. **Ship Information**
   - Ship names you enter
   - Cargo capacity (SCU) values

2. **Contract Data**
   - Collection locations
   - Delivery locations
   - Commodity names
   - SCU amounts
   - Container calculations

3. **Delivery Logs**
   - Dates of completed deliveries
   - Delivery details (locations, commodities, SCU amounts)

**Important:** All of this data is:

- Stored **only** on your device
- **Never** transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers
- **Never** shared with anyone
- Completely **under your control**

### Data We Do NOT Collect

SC-LOADMASTER does **NOT** collect, access, transmit, or store:

- ❌ Personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- ❌ Location data (GPS, IP address, etc.)
- ❌ Device identifiers (UDID, advertising ID, etc.)
- ❌ Usage analytics or app behavior data
- ❌ Crash reports or diagnostic data
- ❌ Any information about your Star Citizen account
- ❌ Any information about your gameplay
- ❌ Contacts, photos, or other device data
- ❌ Any data whatsoever from your device beyond what you explicitly enter into the App

---

## How We Use Your Information

Since all data stays on your device, we don't "use" your information in any way. The App simply:

1. **Stores** the data you enter (ships, contracts, logs)
2. **Displays** that data back to you
3. **Performs calculations** (container requirements) locally on your device

There is **no server communication**, **no data transmission**, and **no external processing** of any kind.

---

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage Only

All data is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's secure storage mechanisms:

- Core Data or similar iOS storage framework
- Protected by iOS security features
- Encrypted by iOS (if you have device encryption enabled)

### Your Control

You have complete control over your data:

- **View:** See all your ships and logs within the App
- **Edit:** Modify or update any information
- **Delete:** Remove individual ships, contracts, or log entries
- **Erase All:** Delete the App to remove all data completely

### Data Backup

If you use iCloud Backup or iTunes Backup, your SC-LOADMASTER data may be included in your device backup. This is controlled by your iOS backup settings, not by the App.

To exclude SC-LOADMASTER from backups:

- Go to iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Backups → [This Device]
- Disable SC-LOADMASTER backup if desired
